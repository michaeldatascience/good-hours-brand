## What these products are

Good Hours devices are for general wellness and relaxation. A red light, a massage gun, and a heated eye mask. They are not a substitute for a doctor.

## Ask first

Talk to a doctor before you use a device if you are pregnant, have an implanted device, or have a health condition. The products are not for children.

## Recovery Light

Wear the goggles. Do not look straight into the LEDs. Do not use the light on broken skin. Skip it if you are sensitive to light. It feels gently warm. It is not a heater. Stop if your skin feels wrong.

## Percussion Mini

This device is for temporary relief of minor muscle aches. Use it on larger muscles. Do not use it on the front of the neck, the head, the spine, or joints. Do not use it on bruises or broken skin. Keep it moving. TBD: max 2 minutes on one spot. It shuts off after 15 minutes.

## Heated Eye Mask

Use it for a wind-down, lying down. It should feel warm, not hot. Stop if it feels too hot. Do not drive or walk around while wearing it. Temperature limits are still being confirmed from a sample.

## Back Belt

The back belt is not for sale and not for use yet.

## If something feels wrong

Stop. Take the device off. If you need a doctor, call one. Then email the support address and tell us what happened. This site cannot help in an emergency.
