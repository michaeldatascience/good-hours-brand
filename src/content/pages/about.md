title: About Good Hours
description: Why Good Hours exists. Evenings back, honest specs, and no subscription.
---

Good Hours is a small line of at-home recovery gadgets. A handheld red light. A heated mini massage gun. A heated eye mask. A back wrap is coming later. The idea is simple: your 15 minutes, at the end of the day.

You buy a device once. The price range is $99 to $299. There is no subscription, no account, and no app. The buttons are on the device. The timer turns it off.

We write specs the way we would want to read them. Wavelengths, weight, battery, what is in the box. If a number is not confirmed yet, the draft of this site says so in yellow. We would rather show a gap than invent a figure.

The people we picture are in the United States, mostly men 35 to 60 who train, sit at a desk, drive, or spend the week in hotels. Women use the same routine. The setting is a home office, a sofa, a garage gym, or a hotel room. Evening light. A warm lamp. Not a clinic and not a gym floor.

What we will not do is dress this up. These are gadgets for a wind-down. They are for general wellness and relaxation. They are not a stand-in for a doctor. If you are pregnant, have an implanted device, or have a health condition, ask your doctor before you start. The products are not for children.

The brand you see is Good Hours. The company name sits in the footer. Questions go to the support email on the contact page. We answer from that inbox. There is no chat bot on this site, and there is no cart here either. This website is a catalog you can read.
