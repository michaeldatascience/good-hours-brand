Three ideas. Red light, warmth, and percussion. This page says what the devices are and how a session goes. It does not promise a result.

## Red and near-infrared light

The Recovery Light is a palm-size aluminum device. It has 12 four-core LEDs and four wavelengths: 630, 660, 830 and 850 nm. Mode 1 is 660 and 850 nm. Mode 2 is 630 and 830 nm. Output at the LED surface is 100 mW/cm².

You charge it on the included dock with USB-C. The battery is 4,000 mAh. One button handles the mode and the timer. The choices are 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes. Then it turns itself off. There is no app.

A session looks like this. Put on the goggles before the LEDs face you. Do not look straight into the light. Rest the device on the area, or hold it a few inches away. The straps let your hand relax. Start with 10 minutes. It feels gently warm. It is not a heater.

Skip it on broken skin, and skip it if you are sensitive to light. Weight and size are still being confirmed from a sample. Those lines are marked on the product page.

## Warmth

The Heated Eye Mask is for the last part of the evening. It combines warmth, gentle air pressure, and vibration. It is thermostat controlled. The temperature, battery, and auto-off time are still being confirmed from a sample, so those rows say so.

Use it lying down, in a dim room. It should feel warm, not hot. Stop if it does not. Do not walk around in it, and do not drive in it.

The Percussion Mini has a separate warm and cool head. Warm is 95 to 113 °F (35 to 45 °C). Cool is 50 to 68 °F (10 to 20 °C). That head is one of five. The other four are standard shapes.

## Percussion

The Percussion Mini weighs 1.4 lb (620 g). The motor is brushless. Three speeds run from 2,800 to 3,200 rpm. Amplitude is 0.31 in (8 mm). The battery is 2,400 mAh, about 3 to 5 hours. It shuts off after 15 minutes. Noise and the final charge port are still open on the spec table.

It is for temporary relief of minor muscle aches. Use it on larger muscles: calves, quads, glutes, and back muscles away from the spine. Keep it moving. Do not use it on the front of the neck, the head, the spine, or joints. Do not use it on bruises or broken skin. A max time per spot is still TBD: 2 minutes, until we confirm the manual.

## Before you start

Pregnant, have an implanted device, or have a health condition? Ask your doctor first. Not for children. Read the safety page and the note in the footer. The routine is short on purpose. Set a timer. Stop when it ends.
