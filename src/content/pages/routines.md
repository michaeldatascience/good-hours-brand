---
title: Routines
description: Four short routines. After training, after a desk day, before bed, and on the road.
---

Pick one. Each routine is a short block of time with a clear stop. Use the devices you own. Skip a step if you do not have that piece.

## After training, 15 minutes

Best with the Percussion Mini and the Recovery Light.

1. Sit down. You are done training. This is not more training.
2. Percussion Mini on large muscles, low speed, a few minutes each area. Stay off the front of the neck, the head, the spine, and joints.
3. Goggles on. Recovery Light, mode 1 or 2, 10 minutes. Strap it on or hold it close.
4. Let the timer finish. Put both devices on their chargers.

## After a long day at the desk, 10 minutes

Best with the Recovery Light. Add the Percussion Mini if your legs feel tight.

1. Leave the desk chair if you can. A sofa is fine.
2. Recovery Light on the shoulders or the legs. 10 minutes. Goggles on.
3. Optional: a short pass with the Percussion Mini on the calves or quads.
4. Stop at 10 minutes even if the inbox is still open.

## Before bed, 20 minutes

Best with the Recovery Light and the Heated Eye Mask.

1. Dim the room. Set the phone face down.
2. Recovery Light for 10 minutes if you want the light. Goggles on.
3. Heated Eye Mask for about 10 minutes. Lie down. Lowest warmth the first night.
4. Take the mask off when it stops. Lights out.

## In the bag

Best with the Recovery Light. Add the Percussion Mini if the carry-on has room.

1. Pack the light, the goggles, and the cable. The travel case holds the light and the mini gun.
2. In the hotel, use the same timer you use at home. 10 or 15 minutes.
3. Check airline battery rules before you fly. That note is still open on the Recovery Light page.
4. The eye mask is the easiest add if the trip is mostly evenings.
